The complete toolkit for bookmarking, routing, and exploration.

How it works

From one dropped pin to the whole journey.

  1. 01

    Save

    Drop a pin anywhere. Keep it forever.

    Center the map on a spot and save it with notes, photos, voice memos, and a rating. Every pin keeps its coordinates, address, and local time zone, even offline.

    • Temporary and persistent flags for quick marks
    • Notes, photos, voice memos, and 1–5 star ratings
  2. 02

    Organize

    Every place, sorted your way.

    Group pins into folders and categories, then browse them as a list, a photo grid, or by folder. Sort by distance to find what’s nearest right now.

    • List, grid, folder, and photo views
    • Import GPX, KML, and CSV; export PDF or CSV
  3. 03

    Route planner

    Optimize multi-stop routes, then track the journey.

    Pick pins from any folder and Maplet builds a driving or walking route between them. Auto-optimize the stop order, check places off as you go, and hand the route to the navigation app you already use.

    • Live distance, ETA, and journey progress tracking
    • Open in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, HERE, Gaode Maps, Baidu Maps, Yandex Maps, and more
  4. 04

    Measure

    Measure distance and area, then walk or drive it.

    Drop points to measure the distance between them, or switch to Area for the size and perimeter of any shape. Tap walk or car to see the real route through your points, then tap Go to continue in your map app.

    • Segment lengths and a running total as you add points
    • Area and perimeter for any shape you outline

Compass Target

Navigate by bearing when you lose signal.

Compass Target is a GPS bearing tracker for the places you saved, plus the Sun, Moon, Qibla, and your parked car. A compass overlay sits on the map, and arrival alerts fire when you enter a 500 m radius of a pin. No cell service required.

  • Offline waypoint navigation by distance and bearing
  • Live compass overlay on the map
  • Arrival alerts even when the app is in the background

maplet.net/share

Share a folder without asking anyone to install the app.

Long-press a folder, choose Share via Deep Link, and Maplet encodes titles, coordinates, notes, and ratings into one short maplet.net/share URL on your device. Recipients get an interactive map in the browser, or Maplet opens if it’s installed.

  • Private deep links. Nothing is uploaded to a server
  • Import GPX, KML, and CSV; export PDF, CSV, or a full .maplet file
  • Open any pin in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, HERE, Gaode Maps, Baidu Maps, Yandex Maps, and more

FAQ

Common questions

What is the best offline route planner for iPhone?
Maplet is an offline route planner for iOS. Save waypoints on your device, optimize multi-stop routes, and keep navigating when there’s no signal.
How do I share a custom map on iPhone?
Long-press a folder in Maplet and choose Share via Deep Link. The app encodes your pins into a maplet.net/share URL on your device. Anyone with the link gets an interactive map. No account, and nothing is uploaded to a server.
Can I measure distance and area on a map?
Yes. Drop points on the map to see the distance between each point and the total, or switch to Area for the size and perimeter of the shape. Maplet can also show a walking or driving route through your points and open it in your map app. Measurements are approximate.
Can I import GPX and KML files?
Yes. Maplet imports GPX and KML files, plus CSV coordinate lists. You can export CSV coordinate data, PDF reports, or a full .maplet archive.
Does Maplet work without internet?
Yes. Saved places, notes, coordinates, and Compass Target (the GPS bearing tracker) all work offline. iCloud sync is optional and the only feature that needs a connection.
