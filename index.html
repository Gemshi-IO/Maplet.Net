Why Maplet

Everything you need to remember everywhere.

Organize & tag

Sort spots into color-coded folders and categories, add notes, ratings, and mark places as visited as you go.

Instant share

Share an entire folder as one ultra-compact link via maplet.net/share — friends see a live map instantly, no app required.

Privacy first

Your data stays on your device. No account creation, no aggressive tracking, no server ever sees your saved places.

Offline ready

Access saved places, notes, and coordinates anytime — on a plane, off-grid, or anywhere signal can't reach.

Trip planning

Plan multi-stop road trips in minutes.

Pick locations from any folder and let Maplet build an optimized driving route between them, complete with distance, duration, and step-by-step stops — then track your progress as you go.

  • Optimized stop ordering for multi-location trips
  • Live distance, ETA, and journey progress tracking
  • Works with Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze

Offline navigation

Find your way, even without signal.

Compass Target points you toward any saved location by distance and bearing — no cell service, no data connection, no problem. Perfect for hiking trails, campgrounds, and remote roadtrips.

  • Real-time distance & bearing to up to 4 locations
  • Fully functional with no internet connection
  • Built-in sunrise, sunset, and elevation readouts

maplet.net/share

Sharing a folder shouldn't require the other person to install anything.

Tap "Share Folder" and Maplet compresses your entire trip — every pin, note, and rating — into one short link. Open it on any phone or computer for an instant, interactive map. If the recipient has Maplet installed, it opens right in the app.

  • Encoded entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded to a server
  • No account or sign-in needed on either end
  • Falls back to a full web preview if the app isn't installed
