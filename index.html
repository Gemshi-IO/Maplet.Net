Why Maplet

A pin log, a route planner, and a GPS toolkit — in one app.

Save & bookmark

Pin any waypoint with a title, notes, photos, and voice memos. Organize into folders, tags, and color-coded categories — with address, distance, and coordinates filled in for you.

Plan multi-stop routes

Build optimized routes for road trips, deliveries, or trails. Reorder stops, track journey progress, and open the result in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, and more.

Compass & GPS tools

Live compass overlay on the map. Compass Target tracks saved places, the Sun, Moon, Qibla, and your parked car — plus arrival alerts when you get near a pin.

Private & offline-first

Works fully offline. Optional iCloud sync stays on your Apple ID. No ads, no account, and your saved places never live on a Maplet server.

Route planner

Optimize multi-stop routes, then track the journey.

Pick pins from any folder and Maplet builds a driving or walking route between them. Auto-optimize the stop order, check places off as you go, and hand the route to the navigation app you already use.

  • Multi-stop routes for road trips, deliveries, or trails
  • Live distance, ETA, and journey progress tracking
  • Open in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, HERE, and more

Compass Target

Navigate by bearing when maps go dark.

Compass Target is a live radar for the places you saved — plus the Sun, Moon, Qibla, and your parked car. A compass overlay sits on the map, and arrival alerts fire when you enter a 500 m radius of a pin. No cell service required.

  • Track saved places, Sun, Moon, Qibla, and parked car together
  • Live compass overlay on the map
  • Arrival alerts even when the app is in the background

maplet.net/share

Share a folder without asking anyone to install the app.

Long-press a folder, choose Share via Deep Link, and Maplet encodes the pins — titles, coordinates, notes, ratings — into one short maplet.net/share URL. Entirely on-device. Recipients get an interactive map in the browser, or Maplet opens if it’s installed.

  • Private deep links — nothing is uploaded to a server
  • Import GPX, KML, and CSV; export PDF, CSV, or a full .maplet file
  • Open any pin in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, and 8+ other apps
